So the steps I did and followed are from lectures. The idea of preprocessing is simple. I cleaned and split the data into english and saved it into english reviews csv. For cnemar test we compare two classifiers on exact same reviews.
SO run the file and do the checksum I will mention the checksum if it matches then we all have the same file and then you can use that to train. 
we know it has multiple languages I filtered for english for 500 True positives, 500 FP, 500 TN, 500 FN. The original index column turned out to restart at 0 for the fake reviews, so every ID appeared twice. I used row number from the original file as the if instead, so every reiew can be traced back.
Each review comes in two parts, what the person liked and what they did not. I mixed them together into one text. Some of those parts are empty, so I filled the empty ones with nothing first. Otherwise pandas literally writes the word "nan" into about 300 reviews, mostly the real ones, and the models would happily learn that "nan" means human.
character normalization so that there is no cnfusion.
I saw a pattern of AI mentioning the hotel name more when compared to the real ones. So I replaced each review's own hotel name with the word hotelname and the city with cityname. The models can still pick up on "this review names the hotel", but they can't just memorise that "beijing" means fake. I only used the hotel and city columns to edit each review's own text, and dropped them right after. Then the sanity check. 
Then the split I did one stratified 75/25 just as siad in the assignment with seed 42. Please ask me why 42😅 I saw something about something called the hitchhikers guide to the galaxy. We can also discuss about the seeds as well if you dont want 42 of them which actually give 1500 reviews for traaining and 500 for testing with TP, TN, FP, FN 125 each. 
For the bag of words if followed what was discussed in the elctures everything lowercased, punctuation and numbers removed. I use single word and word pairings just as in the lecture he told word pairings help. Words that appear less than 1% of training reviews are removed. which the lecture used the same threshold for the bigram model. The matrix contains plain word counts, since the assignment asks for word frequencies. That gives about 1,100 features. The vocabulary is built from the training reviews only, and the test reviews are mapped onto it, the same as the lecture does with its dictionary.
The one place where i deliberately went against the lectures is stopwords. I kept them. In the sentiment analysis, where words like "the" and "we" don't mean anything. Here we're trying to tell how something was written, and those little words are exactly where people and AI differ. Humans write "we", "you" and "so" all the time, while AI likes "also", "were" and "made". throwing those away would throw away the signal.
I also did an ablation study on this as well I can share that as well. if you want. Keeping the stop words showed some improvement in the performance. also I think it is said that too good accuracy is just as bad as too bad is not it? please let me know your idea on this.
